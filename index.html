Frequently Asked Questions

📐 How do I measure my bed or window for a mosquito net?
Measure the complete width, height and length of the area where the mosquito screen will be installed. For custom-size screens, send the measurements to us before ordering.
📐 Do you have nets that fit king-size or super-king beds?
Yes. Size options can be selected based on your bed dimensions. Custom sizes can also be discussed.
📐 Can these nets be used on bunk beds or baby cribs?
The suitable design depends on the exact size and installation location. Please provide the dimensions before purchase.
📐 Do you offer custom-size nets for non-standard window frames?
Yes. Custom-size window mosquito screens can be made according to the required measurements.
🛠️ Do I need to drill holes in the wall or ceiling?
Installation requirements depend on the type of mosquito screen. Some designs require fixing to the frame or wall, while other designs can be installed without permanent drilling.
🛠️ Does the bed net come with a standing frame?
This depends on the selected model. Please contact us with your required bed size and installation type.
🛠️ How do magnetic window mosquito nets attach to the frame?
Magnetic screens generally use magnetic strips or magnetic closures around the opening so the screen can be opened and closed conveniently.
🛠️ Is it easy to take the net down?
Many removable mosquito-screen designs can be taken down when required. The exact method depends on the installation type.
🧵 What material are the nets made from?
Available material can vary by product. Please contact Mr. Royal Mosquito Screens for the material options available.
🧵 Are the nets treated with chemical insecticides?
Treatment depends on the particular product. Please confirm the specification of the selected screen before purchase.
🧵 How small is the mesh size?
Mesh size depends on the selected product. Contact us for the exact mesh specification.
🧵 Is the fabric durable enough for homes with pets?
Durability depends on the selected mesh and frame. Please ask us for a suitable option if you have cats, dogs or other pets.
🧺 Can I wash the mosquito net in a washing machine?
Cleaning instructions depend on the material. Check the product-specific care instructions before machine washing.
🧺 How should I clean a magnetic or roller window screen?
Gently remove dust using a soft brush or vacuum attachment. Follow the care instructions for the particular screen.
🧺 Can I iron the net if it gets wrinkled?
Avoid high heat directly on the net unless the material is specifically confirmed to be safe for ironing.
🧺 What is the best way to store the net?
Clean and completely dry the net before storing it. Keep it folded or rolled in a dry place away from moisture and sharp objects.
🚪 Do you have pop-up mosquito nets?
Product availability depends on the current range. Contact us to check available pop-up or removable models.
🚪 Can mosquito nets be used outdoors?
Some mosquito-screen products can be used outdoors. Suitability depends on the product, installation and weather exposure.
🚪 How does the opening and closure work?
Depending on the design, opening and closure may use zippers, magnets, overlapping mesh or other fixing methods.
🚪 Do window nets block fresh air or the outside view?
Mosquito screens are designed to allow ventilation while helping keep insects outside. Visibility and airflow depend on the mesh design.
📦 What is the return policy if the net doesn't fit?
Please confirm measurements before ordering. For custom-size products, return conditions may differ. Contact us before placing an order for the applicable policy.
📦 Do products come with a warranty?
Warranty availability depends on the product. Please confirm the applicable warranty terms before purchase.
📦 How long does shipping take?
Delivery time depends on product availability, customization and delivery location. Contact us for the current estimate.
